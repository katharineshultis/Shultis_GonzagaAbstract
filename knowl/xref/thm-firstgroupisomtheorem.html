Theorem 16.0.3. First Isomorphism Theorem for Groups.

Let \(G\) and \(H\) be groups and \(\varphi:G\to H\) a group homomorphism. Then \(\ker(\varphi)\) is a normal subgroup of \(G\text{,}\) \(\operatorname{im}(\varphi)\) is a subgroup of \(H\) and
\begin{gather*} G/\ker(\varphi)\cong\operatorname{im}(\varphi)\text{.} \end{gather*}
