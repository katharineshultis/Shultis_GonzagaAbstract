Definition 17.0.7.

A permutation \(\sigma\in S_{n}\) is called even if it can be decomposed as the product of an even number of transpositions and is called odd if it can be decomposed as the product of an odd number of transpositions. The set of all even permutations in \(S_{n}\) is a normal subgroup of \(S_{n}\) called the Alternating Group on \(n\) elements and denoted \(A_{n}\text{.}\)
